Grosse Point Lighthouse Announces Retirement of Executive Director Don Terras After 43 Years of Dedicated Service

Evanston, IL —After 43 years of extraordinary service, Don Terras, Executive Director of the Grosse Point Lighthouse, has announced his upcoming retirement, marking the conclusion of a remarkable career dedicated to preserving one of America’s important historic lighthouses and its rich maritime heritage.

Since joining the staff of Grosse Point Lighthouse in April of 1983, Don has devoted his professional life to the preservation, protection, and interpretation of the Grosse Point Lighthouse. His extensive knowledge of lighthouses throughout the United States, combined with his passion for maritime history and historic preservation, has established him as a national authority on lighthouses serving for six years as president of the American Lighthouse Coordinating Committee and a member of the advisory committee to establish the National Lighthouse Museum in New York City. He is credited with the designation of Grosse Point Lighthouse as a National Historic Landmark and the launch of a Grosse Point Lighthouse Preservation Fund.

Don’s contribution goes well beyond the responsibilities of an Executive Director, serving as lighthouse steward, protecting not only its physical structure but also the history, stories, and its significance. Through his book on Grosse Point Lighthouse and written contributions to other publications, museum exhibit development, lectures, dedication, expertise, and commitment, he has helped ensure that this important piece of American history remains a living part of the Evanston community and an educational resource for future generations.

“Don’s contributions over the past 43 years are difficult to overstate,” said Joe Behles, Park District President. “Stewardship is about taking responsibility for something bigger than yourself and leaving it better for those who follow. Don has embodied that responsibility throughout his career. He has devoted his life to protecting and sharing the legacy of the Grosse Point Lighthouse. His knowledge, passion, and commitment are extraordinary, and we owe him an enormous debt of gratitude.”

The Grosse Point Lighthouse is an important landmark in Evanston and a significant part of the maritime history of the Great Lakes. Don’s leadership has helped preserve not only the lighthouse itself but also an appreciation for the critical role lighthouses played in protecting mariners and guiding commerce along America’s waterways.

As Don prepares to retire, he leaves behind a legacy of stewardship, knowledge, and dedication that will benefit the lighthouse and the community for generations to come.

The Lighthouse Park District is deeply grateful for Don’s remarkable contributions and looks forward to celebrating his career and legacy in the coming months.
